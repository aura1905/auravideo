HOLD MY WHEEL — a co-op demo about keeping one bus alive, and not eating your friends.

▶ Steam demo (free): https://store.steampowered.com/app/5097470/

── The quick version ──
What you do : drive, patch the walls, drag wrecks out of the way with magnetic arrows
Best bit    : winching the whole bus up a cliff, right before it explodes
The catch   : the bus slides like it weighs nothing and the animations are stiff
Demo        : about 3.7 GB, 14 languages
Full game   : 3 Nov 2026
Think       : Pacific Drive, but the car is a bus and your friends are in it
Verdict     : Worth A Dip

Frontier Tale's demo is 2.17 GB. 1.6 GB of it sits in a folder Unity itself names
"BackUpThisFolder_ButDontShipItWithYourGame" — 892 files, including the 577 C++ files
IL2CPP generated from their C#, and 88 debug symbol files (one is 688 MB on its own).

The game is 615 MB. Everything else is a folder that never runs.

Because it shipped, IL2CPP hides nothing here: A* Pathfinding Project, Behavior Designer,
Cinemachine, DOTween, FMOD, Steamworks.NET.
